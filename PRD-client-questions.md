## Possible Questions for Client


- How many people are involved in building a new event? Who are they?
- In the past, have you prepared schedules for the admin teams as well as attendees? (If for admin teams, who?) 
- Do you do postmortems after each event? Do you take notes on how things went to review when planning the next one?
- Have you noticed any pain points that attendees have when following the event schedule?
- In the past, how have you used past data to prepare for future events?
- I heard that you want analysis of past records. Do you have more information on that? Does that include attendance, schedule, duration of sessions…?
- Walk me through how you built your last event schedule. Where did you start?
- Does communication ever break down between the event planners or administrative team? When is it difficult to make progress together?
